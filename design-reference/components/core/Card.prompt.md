The system's surface container. `plain` for most content, `poster` (ink border + 5px hard offset shadow) for the playful, flyer-like moments.

```jsx
<Card variant="poster"><h3>Taco Bell Black Tie</h3><p>Formalwear. Fast food.</p></Card>
```

Max one `poster` card per screen region — it is the loudest surface in the system.
